# Volt Roadster: store listing copy

**Short description (80 chars):** Electric hypercar stunt racing on Earth, the Moon and Mars. Easy classic controls.

**Full description**

Floor it in an electric hypercar and race across Earth, the Moon and Mars. Jump canyons, run loops, dodge meteors on the Moon and dust devils on Mars, and land on glowing pads for big scores.

Controls that feel like a proper mobile racer:
- Classic layout: ◀ ▶ steering on the left, big GAS and BRAKE pedals on the right
- A dedicated R reverse pedal (or hold BRAKE when stopped) with a D/N/R gear indicator
- Thumb-sized boost, drift and jump/thruster buttons
- Prefer analog steering? Switch to the analog pad or tilt in Settings → Controls
- A short controls tutorial the first time you play

Three camera views (close chase, far chase and hood) are one tap away, and the game remembers your pick.

Plus: a Cybertruck-style pickup with cold-gas thrusters, ghost replays, slow-mo jump cam, photo mode, paint and parts customization, daily challenges, base building and a spaceport finale.

Smooth on modern phones, with sharp native-resolution graphics on Ultra.
