# 20 ideas to make Volt Roadster awesome (requirement 11)

1. **Photo mode with orbit cam**: freeze any jump mid-air, add depth of field and planet-themed frames, then share. Pairs with the Paparazzi achievement.
2. **Ghost duels with friends**: share a 6-character code and race a friend's best ghost (ghost recording already exists in `sim.js`).
3. **Bullet-time landings**: time slows to 30% for the last 0.4 s before a precision pad so you can line up a PERFECT.
4. **Roadster in orbit easter egg**: a tiny red roadster with a mannequin drifting across the Moon sky; tapping it in photo mode unlocks a paint.
5. **Weather and time-of-day remix**: replay any Earth level at night, in rain (lower grip, reflections) or in fog for 1.5x coins.
6. **Rocket launch finale**: drive into a rocket at the Earth spaceport to "travel" to the Moon or Mars with a cinematic launch.
7. **Mass Driver stunt**: get catapulted 2 km across the Moon and steer onto a crater bullseye.
8. **Dynamic dust devils on Mars**: ride inside a twister for a spin bonus (ties into the Storm Chaser achievement).
9. **Meteor shower dodge on the Moon**: glowing impact markers warn you 2 s before meteors hit the road.
10. **Level editor (Roadster Builder)**: snap together the same segment types the engine uses, test-drive them, and share them as codes.
11. **Weekly Cup**: a fixed seed and one level for everyone, with a global leaderboard kept in the cloud save.
12. **Haptic signature**: unique vibration patterns for loops, perfect landings, car jumps and battery warnings, tuned for the OnePlus 15's motor.
13. **Adaptive music**: stems that layer in with speed and combo multiplier, plus a drop timed to the moment you leave a ramp.
14. **Robot pit crew cutscene**: your VOLT-Bots swap the battery in 2 seconds at a mid-level pit lane, funny and fast.
15. **Robot customisation**: dress your helpers (hard hats, scarves, an astronaut cape) using cosmetics from quests.
16. **Car-jump traffic chains**: chaining 3+ car jumps without touching the ground triggers "Traffic Surfer" for a x2 combo.
17. **Planet-specific tyres**: cosmetic tyre-mark trails (lunar boot prints, red dust plumes) that also leave tracks on terrain.
18. **Speedrun mode**: split timer at every checkpoint, coloured green/red against your best, with an instant-restart button.
19. **Daily postcard**: after the first finish of the day, the game renders a postcard of your best jump moment.
20. **Accessibility pack**: one-thumb mode (auto-gas plus tilt steering), colour-blind landing-pad rings, and adjustable game speed for younger players.
